The goal is a proper home for intraday traders on Linux, with futures and crypto sharing the same charting and replay workflow.

FUTURES / IN DEVELOPMENT

ES and NQ are part of Alpharch.

The futures workflow is being built around dated contracts, exact tick prices, depth, tape and replay. The read-only Interactive Brokers bridge is implemented and has passed offline checks; testing with a signed-in gateway and exchange permissions is still pending.

Rithmic and CQG need their own adapters and provider access. Live broker execution is not implemented. Futures data subscriptions remain separate.

03 / THE EVERYDAY TOOLS

Spend your attention
on the chart.

Get closer to the tape.

Zoom time around the cursor. Stretch the price scale. Inspect the exact price and exchange timestamp. Switch between candles, heatmap, footprint, profile, ladder and tape.

Keep your studies where you want them.

Search for RSI, MACD, moving averages, Bollinger Bands, stochastic, VWAP or volume and delta tools. Edit the settings and colors, use a separate pane, and save your study template.

Bring your morning together.

The Daily combines your configured crypto watchlist, session clock, calendar and yesterday’s journal debrief. Open it before the session and save a copy to your journal.

About the morning brief →

Save the desk. Replay the session.

Save your Hyprland charts, drawings and studies as a named desk. Record public feeds locally for review. Replay stays local to each Hyprland window; Classic supports several streams on one replay clock.

Let Omarchy do the window work.

Focus, swap, resize and move charts with your Hyprland shortcuts. Add a chart and the desktop tiles it alongside the others. Your workspaces, monitor setup and keyboard habits stay part of the workflow.

04 / OPTIONAL PAID ASSISTANT · PLANNED

Desk Brain.
A second set of eyes.

Keep the core workspace free. Add a hosted AI assistant when you want help bringing the session together.

The paid service is in planning. Pricing and included usage will be published before subscriptions open.

Before the open

A personalized morning brief with available overnight context, the economic calendar and levels from your connected data.

During the session

Updates around the levels you watch, with explanations of confirmed changes and links to the source and timestamp.

After the close

Replay bookmarks, journal summaries and recurring patterns in the sessions you choose to review.

These are planned subscription features. Existing charts, drawings, indicators, local replay, journals and the basic Daily remain free. The current optional Claude CLI integration remains available with your own model account.
