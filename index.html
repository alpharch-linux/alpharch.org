02 / THE EVERYDAY TOOLS

Spend your attention
on the chart.

Get closer to the tape.

Zoom time around the cursor. Stretch the price scale. Inspect the exact price and exchange timestamp. Switch between candles, heatmap, footprint, profile, ladder and tape.

Keep your studies where you want them.

Search for RSI, MACD, moving averages, Bollinger Bands, stochastic, VWAP or volume and delta tools. Edit the settings and colors, use a separate pane, and save your study template.

Bring your morning together.

The Daily combines your configured crypto watchlist, session clock, calendar and yesterday’s journal debrief. Open it before the session and save a copy to your journal.

About the morning brief →

Save the desk. Replay the session.

Keep named layouts with your drawings and studies. Record public-feed sessions locally, replay several recordings on one clock, and return to your live desk when you’re done.

Let Linux do the window work.

Use a tiled page or open independent Omarchy windows. Change the balance between charts without giving up the shortcuts and workspace habits you already have.

03 / KNOW YOUR CONNECTION

Start with your feed.

The development desk reads public Coinbase, Hyperliquid and Kraken data for BTC, ETH and SOL. Deribit supplies the optional crypto options context.

Futures are a separate step. The read-only IBKR bridge has passed offline checks, but still needs testing with a signed-in gateway and exchange permissions. Rithmic and CQG adapters are not implemented yet. There is no live broker execution.

Broker & feed guides ↗

Read what the data can tell you.

Captured VWAP and depth history start with the data the desk has received. A sparse footprint stays sparse. Missing aggressor information stays unknown.

Software is free. Exchange data, provider access and optional model services may have their own costs.
