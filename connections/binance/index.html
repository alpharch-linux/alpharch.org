How to connect

Binance.

Binance publishes spot WebSocket market streams. Alpharch retains legacy Binance spot and perpetual parsers, but connections from this test location returned HTTP 451; the new desk does not offer this feed.

Alpharch supportExisting tool · access limited

The legacy adapter remains in Alpharch, but this feed was not reachable from the test location. It is not offered in the new desk.

Before you start

  • A supported Alpharch adapter for this exchange and product.
  • Regional availability and the provider’s current data-use conditions.

Prepare your connection

  1. Check availability first

    Use Binance only where the service is available to you. Binance and Binance.US are different services.

  2. Check the installed tool’s help

    The legacy alphad tool accepts --exchange binance with --market spot or --market perp. A parser existing in the software does not guarantee a reachable feed.

  3. Respect connection errors

    HTTP 451 is an access restriction, not a reason to change passwords or bypass the restriction. Choose a reachable supported source.

If something is missing

Price differs from another venue

Check the exchange, product, quote currency and contract. Do not substitute another exchange’s price.

Connection unavailable in your region

Use a provider available to you. Do not bypass regional access restrictions.
