How to connect

dxFeed.

dxFeed documents dxLink market-data connections with multiple authorization modes. A demo endpoint is not proof of live exchange entitlement or a working Alpharch adapter.

Alpharch supportIntegration & provider access required

Alpharch still needs an implemented, tested adapter and any required provider approval. Having an account alone does not enable this connection.

Before you start

  • The contracted service endpoint and authentication supplied for your account.
  • The required exchange permissions and an implemented Alpharch adapter.

Prepare your connection

  1. Confirm the service

    Ask which markets, depth and live or delayed permissions are included in the account.

  2. Use the assigned access details

    Endpoint and authorization must match the subscribed service. Credentials issued through another platform may not be portable.

  3. Check Alpharch availability

    No dxFeed adapter is released. Demo examples remain development references, not live production connections.

If something is missing

Demo quotes appear but the contract is unavailable

Verify the service scope and entitlement before treating the feed as live.

Token rejected

Confirm the token’s intended endpoint, scope and expiry with the issuing provider.
