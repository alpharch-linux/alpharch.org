Connections/Futures brokers

How to connect

AMP Futures.

AMP advertises several data and routing choices. Alpharch must support the particular route enabled on your account; an AMP customer-portal login is not a universal market-data login.

Alpharch supportIntegration & provider access required

Alpharch still needs an implemented, tested adapter and any required provider approval. Having an account alone does not enable this connection.

Before you start

  • Your enabled platform/data route and its separate login.
  • Exchange subscriptions appropriate to quotes or depth.

Prepare your connection

  1. Check your AMP platform setup

    Find which data provider was selected for your account. Do not change subscriptions merely to try an unreleased adapter.

  2. Choose the matching guide

    Use the CQG or Rithmic page for those routes. TT and Teton do not have an Alpharch adapter.

  3. Verify access with AMP

    Confirm the requested application and depth permissions before a future connection attempt.

If something is missing

Quotes are available but the ladder is empty

AMP lists top-of-book and depth subscriptions separately. Check the exchange and subscription level.

Connection fails during the weekend

Check the provider’s maintenance status before changing credentials.
