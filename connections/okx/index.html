How to connect

OKX.

OKX documents public and private WebSocket services. Endpoint selection and product availability must match the user’s regional account; Alpharch has no released OKX adapter.

Alpharch supportAdapter not released

Before you start

  • A supported Alpharch adapter for this exchange and product.
  • Regional availability and the provider’s current data-use conditions.

Prepare your connection

  1. Choose the exact market

    Keep spot, perpetuals and dated futures distinct. The same ticker can represent different instruments.

  2. Use public data when supported

    Public market-data channels do not require linking a trading account. Private account features use a separate authorization path.

  3. Check Alpharch availability

    This exchange has not been integrated into Alpharch. The documented API is a development route, not a working connection in the current release.

If something is missing

Price differs from another venue

Check the exchange, product, quote currency and contract. Do not substitute another exchange’s price.

Connection unavailable in your region

Use a provider available to you. Do not bypass regional access restrictions.
