How to connect

Kraken.

Kraken BTC, ETH and SOL USD spot feeds passed live checks in Alpharch’s development desk on September 5, 2026. Kraken’s futures API is separate and is not implemented in this desk.

Alpharch supportLive feed verified · development

Live public data has passed checks in the development desk. This is not yet a claim about the public installer or authenticated account access.

Before you start

  • The development chart desk with Kraken support. The public installer is a separate release.
  • Access to Kraken’s public market-data service from your location. No account login is needed for these charts.

Prepare your connection

  1. Open Connections in the development desk

    Choose Kraken, then BTC, ETH or SOL. New Chart and Markets also offer the Kraken source.

  2. Choose your chart

    The adapter supplies actual trades, a price-level book and historical candles. Check that the chart is labeled Kraken and USD spot.

  3. Check the received data

    The live check confirmed 100 book levels per side and 720 completed one-minute historical candles for each tested market. Captured order-flow history begins at connection; candles do not supply historical aggressor classification.

  4. Keep account access separate

    The optional Kraken account adapter is read-only development code tested for protocol/error handling. It has not been accepted with a real account and is not a released broker connection.

If something is missing

Kraken is missing from New Chart

Your installed version may predate the development desk. This guide does not mean that the public installer has already shipped it.

Expecting Kraken futures or derivatives

This adapter covers USD spot. It does not connect Kraken Futures or transfer a derivatives subscription.

The book becomes unavailable

A failed book-integrity check or stale connection must recover before the chart is treated as current. Do not substitute another venue’s book.
