ALPHARCH.
The Manual · Overlay v1.3 · Front page

Everything, laid out.

If you can paste one line, you can run the whole desk. This page assumes nothing — not even that you've used Linux before.

I.Install

Three steps. About five minutes.

  1. Install Omarchy.

    Alpharch is an overlay: it sits on top of Omarchy, the Arch Linux system it's built for. Download Omarchy's ISO, put it on a USB stick, boot from it, and follow their installer — a clean install runs in about 76 seconds, and their site walks every click. When you land on the desktop, come back here.

  2. Open a terminal.

    Hold SUPER and press RETURN. (SUPER is the key with the logo on it — the "Windows key". You'll use it constantly; on Omarchy it drives everything.) A black window with a blinking cursor appears. That's the terminal. You type commands into it and press Enter.

  3. Paste this line and press Enter.

    ❯ curl -fsSL https://alpharch.org/install | bash

    It fetches Alpharch, installs it, and wires everything up. If it asks for your password (for one small package), type it — nothing appears as you type; that's normal — and press Enter.

    What success looks like: an amber Alpharch logo, a series of green ✓ lines, then "Done. Start here:". If any command afterwards says not found, close the terminal and open a new one — that refreshes its path.

Two finishing touches, typed into the same terminal:

❯ omarchy-theme-set pit # put on The Pit — the house look ❯ alpharch doctor # checks every dependency and data feed The doctor is honest: green means live from your machine, red says exactly why not.

US traders, one expected red line: Binance is geo-blocked from US addresses, so perp liquidations/funding show unreachable. Coinbase spot flow and Deribit options flow run live and free. Nothing in Alpharch fakes a feed it can't reach.

VI.Your files

Plain text. Yours.

Alpharch's whole configuration is three small files you can open in any editor. The installer seeds them once and never overwrites them.

FileWhat lives there
~/.config/alpharch/config.tomlYour defaults: exchange, default_symbol, tick (price grid), bucket (footprint minutes), watchlist, waybar_symbol.
~/.config/alpharch/calendar.txtYour events, one per line: Tue 08:30 CPI or 2026-09-17 14:00 FOMC or * 09:15 premarket review (the * means every day). The brief and the clock count down to them.
~/.config/alpharch/desk.tomlThe six rooms — name, command, and an optional monitor = N to pin a room to a screen.
~/Documents/trading-journal/Your journal — one Markdown file per day, screenshots alongside. Never uploaded, never touched by uninstall.
VII.When something looks wrong

Read this before you worry.

  1. "command not found" right after installing. Open a new terminal (SUPER+RETURN). The old one hasn't refreshed its path yet.
  2. The keys don't respond. Run hyprctl reload in a terminal, then try again.
  3. "Everything opens fullscreen!" It doesn't — that's tiling. One window on a workspace correctly fills it, because there's nothing to share the space with. Open a second window on the same workspace and watch them split. (A window that's genuinely stuck full-width was toggled with Omarchy's SUPER+ALT+F — press it again.)
  4. The footprint looks sparse. It builds from live trades and needs a few minutes of tape. The canvas (SUPER+ALT+V) is the better first look at a fresh session.
  5. Everything is the wrong colors. Alpharch wears your active Omarchy theme. For the house look: omarchy-theme-set pit.
  6. A feed shows unreachable. Run alpharch doctor — it tests each feed from your machine and says exactly what's up. Binance from a US address is expected to fail; Coinbase and Deribit carry the live free tier.
  7. Anything else. alpharch update brings the newest fixes. Issues and code: github.com/alpharch-linux/alpharch.

Leaving cleanly.

❯ ~/.local/share/alpharch/uninstall.sh

Removes every trace — keys, commands, themes, branding restored — and keeps your journal and config, because they were never ours.

VIII.The rules of the house

What this will never do.

  1. No signals, no picks, no AI that trades. These are tools for reading markets. The reading is yours.
  2. No fake data. Real futures depth (CME) and equity options flow (OPRA) are paid feeds; until you plug one in, Alpharch says so plainly instead of approximating and dressing it up.
  3. Nothing phones home. Market data comes in; nothing about you goes out. The one network call Alpharch makes for itself is a once-a-day check for updates.
Linux is for traders.
